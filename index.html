01

Compostable doesn't mean what the packaging usually implies.

Most "compostable" bags need an industrial composting facility — specific heat, specific microbes, specific conditions your garden compost bin will never reach. Ours are certified to EN13432, which is a real, tested, independently verified standard. It is also not a promise that your council collects this material, because most don't, yet.

We could have left that bit out. Most bags do.

02

The bag's home-compostable. What's inside it isn't.

Our bag is certified to OK compost HOME as well as industrial. But dog waste carries pathogens that should never go near a compost heap — so whatever the bag can do, what's inside it belongs in the general waste bin. Every time. And real UK garden heaps rarely hit the temperatures the certificate assumes anyway — certified, not magic.

03

One better bag doesn't undo everything else.

We're not going to tell you that switching bags offsets a flight, or that you're "saving the planet one walk at a time." You're using a slightly better material, made a bit more thoughtfully, from a company that would rather tell you the truth than sell you a feeling.

That's smaller than most marketing promises. It also happens to be accurate.
