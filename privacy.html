This policy explains how Frank, a brand of Smart Products Limited ("we", "us"), handles any personal information you share with us through this website. We keep it short because, honestly, this website collects very little.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, logins, or tracking. The only personal data we receive is what you choose to give us:

  • If you join the launch list — your email address, nothing else. It is stored by our website hosting provider (Netlify) and used to send you a single email when Frank goes on sale. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form and can withdraw at any time by emailing us.
  • If you email us at hello@frankpets.co.uk — your email address, your name (if you give it), and whatever is in your message.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics that identify you, and we do not build profiles about visitors. If that changes in future, we'll update this policy first.

How we use it

We use the information you email us for one thing: to read and reply to you, and to sort out whatever you got in touch about (a question, feedback, or a problem with an order). Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

Where your data goes

Launch-list addresses are held by our hosting provider, Netlify, under its own security and privacy terms; we download them only to send the launch email. Your emails to us are handled by our email provider so we can receive and reply to them. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except where we are legally required to.

Buying our products: this website doesn't sell anything or take payments directly. When Frank launches, sales will be handled through a third-party marketplace, which collects and processes your order and payment details under its own privacy policy, not ours — we never see your payment information.

How long we keep it

We keep launch-list addresses until the launch email has been sent, then delete the list. We keep email correspondence only for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards, then delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just email hello@frankpets.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data — for example, if we add a shop or analytics to this site — we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
